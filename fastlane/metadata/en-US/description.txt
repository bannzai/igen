Write down your worry or what happened today, and a fitting great mind appears in the night sky to encourage you with a quote or proverb, complete with its source.

This is not a chat. You receive a single letter of reply, addressed to you.

■ For moments like these
- You have a worry you cannot easily share with the people around you
- You want words that help you face forward on a low night
- You want to write out what is on your mind and sort through it
- You love quotes and proverbs and want to know where they really come from

■ How it works
1. Write about your day or what is on your mind (voice input supported)
2. Stars gather and a great mind appears in your sky as a constellation
3. A letter arrives: a short note, the quote, its meaning and context, and a closing word

■ Every quote comes with its original text and source
Every quote and proverb in a letter is cited with the work, the passage, and the year. Words from other languages always appear with the original text. We do not use unsourced quotes that circulate online, and we do not use AI-generated quotes. The collection spans East and West, from Seneca and Confucius to Nietzsche, Zeami, and Matsuo Basho.

■ Your star atlas
Great minds you meet through your letters light up as constellations in your night sky. Your sky grows with every letter.

■ Your letters
Look back on your letters day by day. The letter to your past self is always there to reread.

■ Share card
Save or share a vertical card with only the great mind and the quote. Your worry is never included in the card.

■ Pricing
- Free: one letter a day
- Letter ticket: one more letter, just for tonight
- Unlimited plan "Stargazer": unlimited letters and unlimited storage for your atlas and letters
Payment is handled through the App Store and you can cancel anytime.

Dear Socrates is an app for encouragement and does not provide professional support. If a message suggests you are in serious distress, the app shows support resources instead of a letter.
