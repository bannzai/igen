{{ avSmall2 }}SenecaStoic philosopher — c. 4 BC–AD 65

In a world that races, you stopped to look up at the sky. That alone is already an act of wisdom.

Life is long, if you know how to use it.
ORIGINAL (LATIN)Vita, si uti scias, longa est.

Two thousand years ago in Rome, Seneca wrote this for people who complained that life was too short. What we lack is not time, he argued, but the resolve to spend it as our own. The speed of your era is not the measure of your worth.

SOURCE
Seneca, On the Shortness of Life, ch. 2
De Brevitate Vitae, II — Latin, c. AD 49
