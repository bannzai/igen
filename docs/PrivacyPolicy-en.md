# Privacy Policy

This document is an English translation of the Japanese Privacy Policy ([プライバシーポリシー](./PrivacyPolicy-ja.html)), provided for reference. In the event of any discrepancy between the Japanese original and this translation, the Japanese original shall prevail.

bannzai (the "Provider") establishes this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") regarding the handling of user information, including personal information, of users in the service 偉言 (igen; published in English as "Dear Socrates") provided by the Provider (the "Service").

## Handling of Consultation Text

To generate responses — the core function of the Service (selecting a historical figure and quote suited to your concern and generating an encouraging message) — the consultation text you enter is transmitted to and stored on the Provider's servers.

- Consultation text is used to generate responses, to display your consultation history, and to improve the quality of responses through accumulated consultations (personalization)
- To generate responses, consultation text is transmitted to external AI service providers used by the Provider for processing. The recipients and the scope of use are limited to what is necessary to achieve the purposes of use set forth in the Policy
- Consultation text is never provided to third parties for advertising purposes

## Information Collected and Collection Methods

### Anonymous User Identifier

The Service does not require user account registration (email address, password, etc.). To manage your consultation history and purchase status, the Service uses an anonymous identifier automatically generated by the app (an identifier issued by Firebase Authentication anonymous sign-in).

### Consultation Text and Usage History

The consultation text you enter into the Service, the generated responses, and their history.

### Voice Input Data

When you use the voice input function, your speech is processed by Apple's speech recognition (the Speech framework) to convert it into text. As part of this processing, audio data may be sent to and processed on Apple's servers ( https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ ). The converted text is treated as consultation text in accordance with the Policy. The Provider does not obtain or store the audio data itself.

### Purchase Information

When you use the paid features of the Service (consultation tickets and subscriptions), the following information is collected to manage your purchases.

- Payment information processed through Apple's App Store (the Provider does not obtain details of your payment methods such as credit card numbers)
- An anonymous identifier and purchase history necessary for managing purchase status, through the purchase management service RevenueCat ( https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy )

### Usage Information

To improve the quality of the Service, the Provider uses Google Firebase Analytics ( https://policies.google.com/privacy ) to collect information about how the app is used (screen views, interaction events, device type, OS version, etc.). Analytics events never include the body of your consultation text.

### Information Provided When Contacting Us

Information you enter or send when making an inquiry by email or other means (email address, inquiry details, etc.).

## Purposes of Use

- To provide the Service, including generating responses, displaying consultation history, and personalization
- To provide paid features, confirm purchase status, and provide payment-related support
- To improve the quality of the Service and address defects
- To respond to inquiries
- To notify you of changes to the terms, policies, and other matters concerning the Service

## Outsourcing to External Services

The Provider entrusts the processing of user information to the following external services to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use.

- Google Firebase (data storage, authentication, and usage analytics; https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy )
- RevenueCat (purchase status management)
- AI service providers (processing of consultation text to generate responses)
- Apple (speech recognition when using voice input; audio may be processed on Apple's servers; https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ )

## Provision to Third Parties

The Provider will not provide personal information among user information to third parties (including those located outside Japan) without the prior consent of the user, except in the following cases where provision to third parties (including those located outside Japan) is necessary.

- Where the Provider entrusts all or part of the handling of personal information to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use
- Where personal information is provided in connection with business succession due to merger or other reasons
- Where it is necessary to cooperate with a national government agency, a local government, or a party entrusted by either in performing affairs prescribed by laws and regulations, and obtaining the user's consent is likely to impede the performance of such affairs
- Where otherwise permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan (the "APPI") or other laws and regulations

## Disclosure of Personal Information

When the Provider is requested by a user to disclose personal information pursuant to the provisions of the APPI, the Provider will, after confirming that the request was made by the user themselves, disclose the information to the user without delay (if such personal information does not exist, the Provider will notify the user to that effect). However, this does not apply where the Provider is not obligated to disclose under the APPI or other laws and regulations.

## Correction, Deletion, and Suspension of Use of Personal Information

1. If the Provider is requested by a user (1) to correct the content of personal information pursuant to the provisions of the APPI on the grounds that the personal information is untrue, or (2) to suspend the use of personal information pursuant to the provisions of the APPI on the grounds that it is being handled beyond the scope of the publicly announced purposes of use or that it was collected by deception or other wrongful means, the Provider will, after confirming that the request was made by the user themselves, conduct the necessary investigation without delay, and based on the results, correct the content of the personal information or suspend its use, and notify the user to that effect.
2. If the Provider is requested by a user to delete the user's personal information, and the Provider determines that it is necessary to comply with the request, the Provider will, after confirming that the request was made by the user themselves, delete the personal information and notify the user to that effect.
3. The preceding two paragraphs do not apply where the Provider is not obligated to make such corrections, suspend use, or delete personal information under the APPI or other laws and regulations.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Provider will change the Policy as necessary. However, in the case of changes to the Policy that require the user's consent under laws and regulations, the changed Policy shall apply only to users who have consented to the changes by the method prescribed by the Provider. When changing the Policy, the Provider will announce the effective date and content of the changed Policy by displaying it in the app or by other appropriate means, or will notify users.
