# Disclosure under the Specified Commercial Transactions Act

This document is an English translation of the disclosure required under the Specified Commercial Transactions Act of Japan ([特定商取引法に基づく表示](./SpecifiedCommercialTransactionAct-ja.html)), provided for reference. In the event of any discrepancy between the Japanese original and this translation, the Japanese original shall prevail.

## Sales Price of Products and Consideration for Services:

Displayed on screen during the purchase process.

## Costs Other Than the Price:

Communication charges for the internet connection necessary to use the service are borne by the user (the amount depends on your carrier's contract).

## Payment Method:

Payment methods provided by the company that processes payments for Apple purchases.

## Timing of Payment:

Consumable items (such as consultation tickets) are charged upon completion of the purchase process. Subscriptions are charged upon completion of the initial purchase process, and thereafter upon renewal of the contract period unless you cancel automatic renewal. The actual timing of billing follows the rules of Apple's App Store.

## Timing of Service Provision:

Available immediately after the purchase is completed.

## Special Provisions on Returns and Cancellations:

Due to the nature of the products, consumable items (such as consultation tickets) cannot be returned or cancelled at the user's convenience after purchase.

For subscriptions, you can stop automatic renewal at any time from your iOS settings (subscription management in your Apple account). Stopping automatic renewal takes effect at the end of the current contract period, and no refund will be made for contract periods already paid.

## System Requirements:

An iOS device on which the app runs (iOS 17.0 or later).
